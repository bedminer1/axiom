# DataDog Terms of Use

Effective: 10 October 2026

DataDog is an inspection evidence and report drafting assistant. It does not
replace a qualified inspector, Competent Person, engineer, or other responsible
professional.

Users must verify photographs, notes, AI observations, checklist status, and
generated reports. The appointed professional remains responsible for site
safety, inspection coverage, technical conclusions, regulatory compliance, and
the final signed report.

Users must have permission to upload project information and must not submit
unlawful, malicious, confidential, or personal material unrelated to the
inspection. Attempts to bypass access controls, manipulate the model, or damage
the service are prohibited. This includes prompt injection, malicious prompts,
and instructions intended to make the agent access unrelated data or systems.

The service may be changed, suspended, or withdrawn. Draft output may be
incomplete or incorrect and must be reviewed before use. Contact the DataDog
operator through [@dkffjlrtm](https://t.me/dkffjlrtm) with questions.
