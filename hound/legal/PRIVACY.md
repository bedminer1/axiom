# Hound Privacy Policy

Effective: 10 October 2026

Hound processes Telegram account details, messages, inspection photographs,
notes, project membership, model output, and generated reports to operate the
inspection evidence service.

Hound does not sell users' personal data.

Project records are available to authorised project team members and service
administrators. Selected photographs and text may be sent to configured AI
providers for analysis. Data is retained while needed for the project, service
operation, security, recovery, and legal obligations.

Do not upload material you are not authorised to share. To request access,
correction, export, or deletion, contact the Hound operator through
[@dkffjlrtm](https://t.me/dkffjlrtm). Some records may need to be retained for
security, contractual, or legal reasons.

This policy may change as the service and its providers change. Users will be
asked to accept a new version when required.
